# Archetype: creative-portfolio (desainer / fotografer / musisi)

## matches

- desainer grafis, fotografer, musisi, ilustrator, videographer, art director
- any individual creative whose value is the body of work, not a product SKU

## recommended_sections

- Gallery / selected works — the portfolio IS the product; curated pieces are the headline, not a bio.
- Services / commission scope — what the creative takes on (branding, wedding shoot, scoring) with scope per offering.
- Contact / brief CTA — the lead starts as a brief, not a checkout; the path is WA or a form.
- About / practice statement — short, one paragraph; the work speaks louder than the bio.

## avoid_sections

- Fabricated client logos — never invent a logo wall; if real clients can't be named, show selected works instead.
- Generic 3-feature grid — "Kreatif, Profesional, Tepat Waktu" says nothing about the craft.
- Tiered pricing table — creative work is scoped per brief, not sold as SKU tiers.

## page_count

- landing: one page. `marketing_site` only when the portfolio has multiple disciplines (e.g. photography vs. motion) each justifying a gallery page.

## cta_logic

- Primary: "Lihat portofolio" or "Konsultasi via WA". Secondary: a per-service inquiry CTA only when the discipline has fixed scope (e.g. a wedding photography package).

## visual_hooks

- Work-first hero: a recent piece as the first visual, not a portrait of the creative.
- Gallery grid with piece title + medium, not anonymous thumbnails.
- A small tools/medium strip (Figma, Procreate, 35mm) for credibility, not as a fabricated client logo wall.

## example_structure

Header (name + discipline) → Gallery (selected works) → Services / commission scope → About (one paragraph) → Contact CTA. Depart when justified — a photographer may lead with one signature image as full-bleed hero before the grid.
