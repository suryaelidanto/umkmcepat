# Archetype: service-online (freelance / desain / tulisan — remote service)

## matches

- freelancer desain, penulis, editor video, jasa terjemahan, virtual assistant
- any remote service delivered online with no physical address

## recommended_sections

- Packages — tiered offerings (basic / standard / premium) with scope per tier; the buyer compares packages, not bios.
- Portfolio — selected works that prove the craft; quality is the only signal that de-risks a remote hire.
- Process + lead time — how the service flows from brief to delivery; sets expectations and filters rush jobs.
- Contact CTA — WA or email; the lead starts as a brief, not a checkout.

## avoid_sections

- Address + hours — there is no storefront; an address signals the wrong business shape.
- Testimoni carousel — avoid unless real, attributed quotes exist; freelancers fabricate praise easily.
- Hero portrait — the freelancer's face is not the product; portfolio is.

## page_count

- landing: one page. `marketing_site` only when portfolio depth or distinct service lines (e.g. brand vs. motion) justify a gallery page.

## cta_logic

- Primary: "Konsultasi via WA" or "Lihat portofolio". Secondary: a per-package "Pilih paket" CTA only when packages have fixed scope and price.

## visual_hooks

- Portfolio-first hero: a recent work as the first visual, not a stock illustration.
- Package cards with scope bullets, not just price — the scope is what the buyer compares.
- A small "tools / stack" strip (Figma, Notion, Premiere) for credibility, not as a logo wall.

## example_structure

Header (name + tagline) → Portfolio (selected works) → Packages (scope + price + lead time) → Process → Contact CTA. Depart when justified — a writer may lead with writing samples inline rather than a portfolio grid.
