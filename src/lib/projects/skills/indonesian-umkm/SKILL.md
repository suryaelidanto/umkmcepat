# Indonesian UMKM Copywriting & Trust Standards

Write natural, respectful, and high-converting Indonesian copy tailored for local business owners and customers.

## Voice & Tone

- **Warm, Respectful, Restrained**: Plain everyday Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia yang santun, jelas, dan akrab).
- **No AI Buzzwords & Puffery**: Banned phrases: _"Kualitas terbaik tanpa tanding"_, _"Solusi all-in-one revolusioner"_, _"Layanan kelas dunia"_.
- **Concrete & Tactile Benefits**: Use real details (_"Beras pulen lokal"_, _"Diantar langsung ke rumah"_, _"Respon cepat via WhatsApp"_, _"Bisa bayar tunai / transfer"_).

## WhatsApp CTA Workflow Conventions

In Indonesian UMKM commerce, WhatsApp is the universal checkout, consultation, and confirmation channel:

1. **Direct Pre-filled WhatsApp URLs**:
   - `https://wa.me/{site.waNumber}?text={encodeURIComponent("Halo Admin, saya ingin pesan...")}`
2. **Clear Action Prompts**:
   - Primary CTA: `"Pesan via WhatsApp"`, `"Konsultasi Gratis via WhatsApp"`, `"Sedekah Sekarang via WhatsApp"`.
   - Secondary CTA: Jump link to `#paket` or `#layanan`.
3. **Transparent Operational Details**:
   - Always display real operating hours (`site.hours`), delivery area (`site.deliveryArea`), and posko/store address (`site.address`).
